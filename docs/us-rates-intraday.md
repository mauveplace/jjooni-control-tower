# US yield intraday observations

The Rates panel provides a separate intraday chart for Cboe 13-week, 5-year,
10-year and 30-year Treasury yield indices distributed through Yahoo Finance.
These are market yield indices in percent, not Treasury official daily curve
observations. The 13-week series is a discount-yield index. This source has no
equivalent two-year index; a separate TradingView two-year market-yield link is
provided without substituting a futures price.

The date selector filters calendar days in Asia/Seoul. A Korean day can contain
parts of two US sessions. Missing periods longer than 15 minutes are gaps, and
points are never generated by interpolation. The bp view uses the first actual
observation on the selected date: `(last yield - first yield) * 100`.

The collector requests five days of five-minute bars, validates symbol identity,
timestamps and yield units, and merges overlapping observations into 30 calendar
days of retained data. It stores observation clocks separately from collection
clocks. Empty selected days stay empty; the Recent data day control selects the
last actual source date. Provider lag is visible through the last observation.

Collection runs independently at minutes 17 and 47 UTC on weekdays (GitHub
schedules are best effort) and from the existing manual market refresh workflow.
Reading the panel reloads published JSON. A failed tenor retains its previous
points with STALE state; healthy tenors still publish. The browser never calls
the upstream quote API or holds a GitHub credential.
